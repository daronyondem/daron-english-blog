# About

AI is fundamentally reshaping how organizations think, decide, delegate, and work. I help senior engineering and technology leaders navigate that transformation.

I draw on 20+ years of solution architecture experience, leadership research, and firsthand experience inside both Microsoft and AWS. My forthcoming book, *Agentic Organizations: How AI Transforms Teams, Culture, and Work*, introduces a framework built on three pillars — **Culture**, **Communication**, and **Delegation** — for leading organizations through the AI transformation that's already underway.

## Background

I currently work on Multicloud AI Strategy at **AWS**, focused on enterprise AI adoption. Before that, I spent years as a **Tech Lead at Microsoft**, covering AI and application innovation across 109 countries. I've also been a startup **CTO** twice, building and shipping global SaaS products.

My academic work bridges technology and leadership. I hold a **PhD in Leadership for Information Systems** from the University of the Cumberlands, where my research examined how technical community engagement impacts engineering leadership and employee engagement. I also hold an MS in Computer Science, an MEng in Engineering Management, and an MA in Organizational Leadership.
